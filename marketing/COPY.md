Every number here comes from the game itself (`convex/targets.json`, `shared/types.ts`, the lobby settings). ## Submission title

Sketchy: one of you can't see the whole picture.

## Short description (about 50 words)

Sketchy is a live party game where everyone recreates a picture by writing AI prompts. One imposter sees the picture with the key detail erased and has to bluff. Compare the AI images, chat, vote, and get scored by an AI judge. 2–8 players, room code, no login, phone or laptop.

## Long description (about 230 words)

Sketchy is a multiplayer party game about prompting. Everyone gets the same target picture and recreates it with an AI image model. First a quick draft of up to 8 words: the AI draws it, scores it out of 100 and tells you what it missed, with one tip. Then you write a final prompt of up to 30 words. Only the final counts.

The twist: one player is the imposter. Their copy of the picture has the key detail erased under a grey "?" cloud, so they have to guess what is there and prompt as if they knew. When the images are revealed, everyone compares them, argues in the chat and votes. A caught imposter can still steal the round by guessing what was hidden from four options.

An AI judge scores every final image in five areas (subject, details, style, color, composition) and every score earns a tier: Gold 85+, Silver 75+, Bronze 60+. Round rules rotate between Warm-up, Taboo (obvious words banned) and Style.

It runs in any browser with a room code or QR: no login, no install. 2–8 players, and bots fill empty seats; two players get a head-to-head with a bot imposter. Alone, play solo against bots or the daily target, keep a streak, send a challenge link that ends in a side-by-side duel, and track friends on a crew board.

Judges: tap "Play solo vs. bots" to play two rounds in about three minutes.
